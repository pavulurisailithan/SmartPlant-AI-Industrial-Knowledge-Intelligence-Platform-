MAINTENANCE REPORT
Report No: MR-2024-089
Date: March 10, 2024
Asset: Pump P-201
Location: Unit 3, Process Area
Engineer: S. Patel
Supervisor: R. Kumar

FAILURE DESCRIPTION:
Pump P-201 tripped on high vibration alarm at 06:45 hrs.
Vibration reading: 6.8 mm/s (Alarm: 4.5 mm/s, Trip: 7.0 mm/s)
Bearing temperature: 92°C (Limit: 80°C)

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS:
Primary Cause: Bearing failure (Drive End)
Contributing Factors:
1. Lubrication interval overdue by 120 hours
2. Previous seal failure (Jan 2024) may have contaminated bearing grease
3. Slight misalignment detected (0.08mm - above tolerance of 0.05mm)

WORK PERFORMED:
1. Isolated pump and followed LOTO procedure
2. Removed bearing housing
3. Replaced Drive End bearing (SKF-6205-2RS)
4. Cleaned and repacked with fresh grease (Shell Gadus S2 V220)
5. Realigned pump to motor (final reading: 0.03mm)
6. Replaced mechanical seal as precaution
7. Recommissioned and monitored for 2 hours

POST-MAINTENANCE READINGS:
Vibration: 2.1 mm/s (Normal)
Bearing Temperature: 58°C (Normal)
Flow: 248 m3/hr (Design: 250 m3/hr)

RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Reduce lubrication interval to 400 hours
2. Install continuous vibration monitoring
3. Schedule alignment check every 6 months
4. Review seal flush system design

PARTS USED:
- SKF-6205-2RS Bearing x1
- JohnCrane-1 Mechanical Seal x1
- Shell Gadus S2 V220 Grease 500g

DOWNTIME: 8 hours
MAINTENANCE COST: ₹45,000
