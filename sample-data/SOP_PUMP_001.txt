Title: Centrifugal Pump Startup and Shutdown Procedure

1. SCOPE
This SOP applies to all centrifugal pumps in Unit 3 process area.

2. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
- Wear PPE: Safety glasses, gloves, safety shoes, hard hat
- Ensure area is clear of personnel before startup
- Check for any ongoing maintenance work (PTW system)
- Never bypass safety interlocks

3. PRE-START CHECKLIST
□ All maintenance work completed and PTW closed
□ Isolation valves in correct position (suction open, discharge closed)
□ Mechanical seal flush flowing (check sight glass)
□ Lube oil level adequate (between min/max marks)
□ Coupling guard in place and secured
□ Instrumentation functional (pressure gauges, flow meter)
□ Motor rotation direction verified (bump test)

4. STARTUP PROCEDURE
Step 1: Open suction valve fully
Step 2: Crack open recirculation valve (if applicable)
Step 3: Start motor using local/remote start button
Step 4: Verify motor starts and reaches full speed (< 10 seconds)
Step 5: Slowly open discharge valve over 30 seconds
Step 6: Close recirculation valve once flow established
Step 7: Verify operating parameters:
  - Flow rate: Design ± 10%
  - Discharge pressure: Design ± 5%
  - Vibration: < 4.5 mm/s
  - Bearing temperature: < 80°C
  - No visible leaks

5. NORMAL SHUTDOWN
Step 1: Slowly close discharge valve
Step 2: Stop motor
Step 3: Close suction valve (if required)
Step 4: Log shutdown in DCS/logbook

6. EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN
Press red ESD button at local panel OR
Activate from DCS emergency shutdown screen
Notify control room immediately

7. TROUBLESHOOTING
Problem: Pump not starting
- Check motor protection relay
- Check power supply
- Check for mechanical seizure

Problem: Low flow/pressure
- Check suction valve position
- Check for cavitation (noise)
- Check impeller condition

Problem: High vibration
- Stop pump immediately
- Check alignment
- Check bearing condition
- Contact maintenance

8. RECORDS
Log all startups and shutdowns in the pump logbook.
Report any abnormalities to shift supervisor.
