PUMP P-201 OEM MAINTENANCE MANUAL
Section 4.3 - Bearing Maintenance

1. INTRODUCTION
Pump P-201 is a centrifugal pump designed for continuous operation in process service.
Model: API 610 BB2, Flow: 250 m3/hr, Head: 85m, Speed: 1450 RPM

2. BEARING SPECIFICATIONS
- Drive End Bearing: SKF-6205-2RS (Deep Groove Ball Bearing)
- Non-Drive End Bearing: SKF-6204-2RS
- Bearing Temperature Limit: 80°C maximum
- Vibration Limit: 4.5 mm/s RMS

3. MAINTENANCE INTERVALS
- Lubrication: Every 500 operating hours or 3 months (whichever is earlier)
- Bearing Inspection: Every 2000 hours
- Bearing Replacement: Every 8000 hours or on condition

4. FAILURE MODES
Common failure modes for this pump:
a) Bearing failure - caused by inadequate lubrication, misalignment, or overloading
b) Mechanical seal failure - caused by dry running, excessive vibration, or contamination
c) Impeller wear - caused by abrasive particles or cavitation

5. TROUBLESHOOTING
High Vibration (>4.5 mm/s):
- Check shaft alignment (tolerance: ±0.05mm)
- Check bearing condition
- Check for cavitation (NPSH available vs required)
- Check for recirculation

High Temperature (>80°C):
- Check lubrication level and quality
- Check cooling water flow
- Check bearing clearances

6. SPARE PARTS LIST
Part No: SKF-6205-2RS - Drive End Bearing
Part No: SKF-6204-2RS - Non-Drive End Bearing
Part No: JohnCrane-1 - Mechanical Seal
Part No: SS316-IMP-250 - Impeller

7. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
- Always isolate and depressurize before maintenance
- Follow LOTO procedure (SOP-SAFE-001)
- Wear appropriate PPE
- Never run pump dry
